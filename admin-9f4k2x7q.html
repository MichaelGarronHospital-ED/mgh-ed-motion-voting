What the flags mean

Needs review: the same name voted from two or more devices. Shared device: one device voted under more than one name. Submissions is how many times a flagged person changed their vote; it is still one ballot in the tally.

Physician summary

  • Full vote (Group 1, 1 point): active staff, 8 or more shifts a month
  • Half vote (Group 2, ½ point): active under 8 shifts, provisional active, active secondary site
  • No vote (Courtesy, 0): recorded but not counted
